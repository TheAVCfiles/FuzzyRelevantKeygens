# AUTOGRAPHY — Locked Brief

**Track:** Replit · **Deadline:** Sept 9, 2026, 2:00 PM PT (work to Sept 7)
**Required:** Gemini + Google Cloud Agent Builder, built with Replit Agent, deployed to `*.replit.app`

---

## One line

**Autography is the signature that writes itself.** When an episode airs and the internet turns, it reads the flood, separates coordinated amplification from authentic audience concern, stages three possible responses, and issues only what the person actually signs — sealed, numbered, and verifiable.

## The anti-autobiography

An autobiography is a person reconstructing their record after the damage. Autography is the record accruing as it happens, in the window where it still matters.

## The question it answers

> Is the internet genuinely turning on me, or am I trapped inside an amplified slice of it — and what should I release before the window closes?

## Who it's for

Reality/unscripted talent and creator-native personalities in the 48 hours after an episode airs. The person whose identity generated the market and who has no owned mechanism to capture it.

## Non-negotiables

1. **The model never decides.** Gemini reads, clusters, reconciles, and drafts. A deterministic policy engine gates. A human signs. No exceptions, no "auto-approve for trusted brands."
2. **Per-artifact scope.** She signs the *thing*, not permission to make things. Blanket consent is the failure mode this product exists to prevent.
3. **Never claims majority opinion.** Platform data is not a poll. Report concentration, coordination likelihood, source diversity, and uncertainty. Never "the public thinks."
4. **Coordination is a likelihood, never a verdict.** Show the signals that produced it.
5. **She never sees a raw hate feed.** The agent reads it so she doesn't have to. Relief, not score.
6. **Entirely synthetic.** Invented show, invented cast, invented flood. No real person, brand, logo, or footage anywhere in the build or the video. This is a rules requirement and a thesis requirement.
7. **Declared, never observed.** Any location or context data is issued by her, not detected about her.
