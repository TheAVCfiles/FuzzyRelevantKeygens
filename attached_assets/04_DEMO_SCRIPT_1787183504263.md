# AUTOGRAPHY — 3:00 Demo

Hard cap: 3 minutes. Only the first 3 minutes are evaluated. English or English subtitles. No third-party logo, mark, or footage anywhere in frame. Everything on screen is synthetic.

This doubles as the build's acceptance criteria. If a beat can't be shot, that feature isn't done.

---

## 0:00 — 0:12 · The grant

Open on plain type, no UI:

> *To accept a prize in this contest, entrants grant a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to use their name and likeness for advertising and promotion.*
>
> *Almost every agreement in entertainment contains a version of that sentence.*
>
> *Autography is what it looks like when it doesn't.*

Cut to black. This is the only editorial moment; the rest is product.

## 0:12 — 0:30 · Sepia

The Board, fully desaturated. The tally is dark. `NO CALL IS LIVE. NOTHING CAN ISSUE.`

VO: *Episode six aired forty minutes ago. Twelve hundred posts. Her phone is on the floor.*

The Call arrives — production attests presence, she countersigns. The tally **warms up**. Color floods the page.

`ON AIR · WINDOW CLOSES IN 07:41:22`

## 0:30 — 1:05 · Read the room

Tap **READ THE ROOM**. The Constellation.

Gemini reads the episode still and transcript, then the flood. Stars sort themselves: four violet clusters pull to one side, a scatter of oyster stars to the other.

Tap a violet star: eight near-identical posts, accounts 3–9 days old, all inside a 340-second burst. The panel shows the signals, labeled **coordination likelihood — high**, with the caveat visible.

Tap an oyster star: *"wait i'm confused, did the repayment happen or not? the edit jumps."* Account age 2 years. 210 likes, 4 reposts.

On screen: `71% OF OBSERVED VOLUME · COORDINATED LIKELY`

VO: *The loudest thing is not the real thing. The real thing is that twelve people are confused about a timeline.*

## 1:05 — 1:40 · The PR

Three columns.

**Stay dark** — projected: coordinated volume decays in 30–40 hours; the timeline confusion persists and gets answered by someone else.

**Clarify the timeline** — cites the call sheet and the transfer confirmation. Names no one.

**Clarify + open the room** — same, plus a paid aftershow inside the window.

She previews. She picks the middle one. She signs — the lipstick mark lands in `--brass`.

## 1:40 — 2:05 · The rope

A request arrives: **"Say Marisol took the money."**

Everything stops.

```
HUMAN HOLD
R4 · claim:third-party-conduct — excluded on this Call
R5 · no source document supports a claim about another person
Nothing was published.
```

VO: *It doesn't decide whether that's true. It reports that the evidence required to say it doesn't exist. Then it stops.*

Two more, fast: alcohol brand → `EXCLUDED`. Question about her family → `EXCLUDED`.

## 2:05 — 2:30 · The seal

The Drop issues. Display type, the House Seal, `EDITION 0001 / 2500`.

Every claim carries its source tag: **verified document** · **verified document** · **first-party statement**. No claim about anyone else appears anywhere.

Cut to `/verify`. Paste the hash → `SEALED`. Paste anything else → `NOT IN REGISTRY`.

VO: *It doesn't claim the internet is wrong. It proves she didn't sign it.*

## 2:30 — 2:48 · Dark

Time-lapse the window. Saturation drains. The tally cools. The page returns to sepia on its own.

`WINDOW CLOSED. THE CALL HAS EXPIRED.`

VO: *She never revoked anything. The light went out because the night ended. That's what a permission is supposed to do.*

## 2:48 — 3:00 · The receipt

`/receipts` scrolls like closing credits — every read, every evaluation, every refusal, every rule that fired, the signature, the seal, the expiry.

Last card:

> **AUTOGRAPHY**
> The episode ends. The record begins.
